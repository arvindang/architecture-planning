# Hydepark imagery & outlines — method and limits

Generated by `scripts/prepare-hydepark-imagery.py` (`--acquire` re-fetches; otherwise it rebuilds from the gitignored
`sources/hydepark/cache/`). Outputs: `assets/sites/hydepark/aerial-*.jpg`, `sources/hydepark/site-geometry.geojson`
(EPSG:26916), `sources/hydepark/imagery/acquisition.json` (requests, sha256) and `hydepark-imagery-data.js`
(`SITE_HYDEPARK_IMAGERY`, local metres from origin [450532, 4627452], x east, y north).

## Imagery

Cook County orthophotography, one ImageServer per flight year (`gis.cookcountyil.gov/imagery/rest/services/CookOrtho<year>`),
6 in (0.152 m) four-band. Clips are exported by the server straight into EPSG:26916 at 0.15 m, 160 × 160 m around the origin;
the context clip is 400 × 400 m at 0.25 m.

| Image | Content | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2025 | Vacant lot, leaf-on | Latest flight |
| 2024 | Vacant lot, leaf-off | Clearest ground; used for the offset check |
| 2020 | Both frame houses standing, leaf-off | Pre-demolition record (the 2022 flight still shows them, leaf-on; 2023 is the first vacant flight) |
| 2025 context | Block 29, Kimbark Ave, Nichols Park, E 54th St | Context |

The services publish no flight date; leaf state shows spring flights. 2024–25 are compiled in NAD83(2011) State Plane, earlier
years in NAD83 State Plane. The server reprojects to NAD83 / UTM 16N and the parcels come through the same server, so relative
alignment is not affected. Absolute position may still carry a few decimetres of datum handling. These are ordinary
orthophotos, not true orthos: roofs lean away from the photo centre, so roof edges are not wall lines.

| Edge | Evidence | Plat-implied line vs county line |

| South | 5422 roof edge (2020, 2024, 2025 agree to ~0.1 m) | 0.3–0.4 m north of county |

| Rear | 5422 garage west face | ~0.3 m east of county |
| North | 5418 eave/shadow edge (2020) | 0 to 0.4 m north of county |
| North | Alley pavement south edge (2024) | 0.7–0.9 m north of county |

County GIS agrees with the visible ground and building evidence to about **±0.5 m**. The centred plat lot fits the south, front
and rear evidence to ~0.1–0.2 m. The north line is the open question. The imagery puts the occupied width between the 5422
building and the alley pavement at about 13.1 m (43 ft), against 40 ft of record lot. That is consistent with the GIS width
(41.8 ft) and with HP-003 (171.51 vs 175.00 ft). Roof cornices and building lean (0.2–0.5 m) cover part of the gap, but they
do not explain it all. A combined boundary survey is the only way to settle it.

Expected error budget for anything drawn here: county GIS ±0.5–1 m; imagery georeferencing ±0.3 m (consistent between flights
to ~0.1 m); building-edge readings ±0.3–0.5 m; plat placement inherits the county error. For design, treat every line as ±1 m
until the survey arrives.
